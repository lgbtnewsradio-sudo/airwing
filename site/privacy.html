Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026. This policy describes the current AirWing 1.2 application.

Content you choose to share

AirWing captures the screen, window, region, media file or system audio selected in the app. The default display may begin capture automatically when the app opens. An active capture indicator appears in the app, and the tray provides Stop mirroring. Closing the window may leave capture running in the tray. Stop capture or quit the app to end it.

Selected content is sent to the receivers you connect, or to authorized browser viewers on your local network. Browser access can require a code. Content may include personal information, notifications, images, documents and audio. AirWing does not automatically upload this content to developer-operated servers.

Local network and security

AirWing uses local network discovery and addresses to find and communicate with receivers. Casting is intended for trusted networks. AirPlay and Cast mirroring use encrypted media transports; local HTTP/browser playback is not encrypted. Do not expose AirWing’s local server to the internet. Receiver manufacturers and other apps have their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your PC

Settings, recent receiver names and network addresses, and diagnostic logs are stored locally. AirPlay pairing keys are encrypted using Windows account-protected storage. They are not included in support exports. Windows account protection does not protect against all software already running as you. Forget pairing removes a receiver’s stored keys. Uninstalling may retain local app data; you can delete it from the app’s Windows user-data directory.

Diagnostics and support

There is no built-in analytics collection or automatic support upload. A support export is initiated by you and writes a local JSON file containing app/runtime versions, preferences, session health and recent logs. Known tokens, pairing secrets, usernames and selected media paths are redacted.

Local network addresses, device names and arbitrary text logged by devices may remain. Review the file before sharing. If you submit it to a public issue tracker, its contents may become public. Do not include private media or unredacted secrets.

External services

External links open in your browser and are subject to the destination’s privacy policy. Store installation and updates are managed by Microsoft. The optional virtual-display driver is a separate third-party product, not bundled or installed by AirWing. This landing page does not include analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or embedded third-party media. GitHub hosts the page and processes web requests under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Choose what to capture and which receivers to connect. Stop mirroring or quit to end capture. Enable access codes for browser viewers on shared networks. Use Forget pairing to remove saved receiver keys. Export and share support information only when you choose.

Contact

For questions or privacy concerns, contact Black Dog Developers, LLC through the AirWing issue tracker. Share only the information necessary to describe your concern; public issues are visible to others.
